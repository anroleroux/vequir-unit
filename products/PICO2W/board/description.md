The Raspberry Pi Pico 2 W microcontroller board: an RP2350 (dual Arm
Cortex-M33 or Hazard3 RISC-V cores at 150 MHz, 520 KB SRAM), 4 MB of flash
and a CYW43439 radio for 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, on a 51 x 21 mm,
1 mm thick board. A micro-USB port at one end powers and programs it, and a
BOOTSEL button beside it puts it in USB mass-storage mode. Twenty pins run
down each long edge at 2.54 mm pitch, the rows 17.78 mm apart, each a
plated through-hole for a header and a castellated half-hole on the edge so
the board can be soldered flat. Four 2.1 mm mounting holes sit 47 mm apart
along the board and 11.4 mm apart across it. Three SWD debug pads and the
wireless module under its metal shield sit towards the far end, with the
antenna at the very end.
