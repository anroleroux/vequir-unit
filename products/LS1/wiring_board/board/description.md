A piece of perfboard (stripless, 0.1 in grid) cut to 14 by 10 holes, for
the LS1's small parts: the three 1N5822 diodes lying in a column along one
end, the three resistors beside them, and the BC337 transistor below the
resistors. The holes for the diodes' thick leads are drilled out, and the
four corner holes are drilled out for M1.2 screws that fix the board to
pillars in the base.
