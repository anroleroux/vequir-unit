A lid that fits on top of the base box (LS1/base), like the PB6 lid. It
extends past the box's breadth by its own thickness on each side and runs
down the outside of the box to the bottom of the base. In the longitudinal
direction it extends a = 5 mm past the box at both ends. Under the lid, four
blocks sit just inside the base's end walls, one at each end of each wall,
where the sides meet them; nothing runs across between them. An M1.2
countersunk screw through the end wall from the outside of the base fastens
each block.

An 8 x 1 RGB WS2812 stick sits against the inside of the lid's +y side,
along it and centred on it, with its LEDs facing out, each LED behind its
own square window through the side. It is screwed to two little pillars on the inside of the
side with M1.2 x 6 countersunk screws.

The lid's -y side has a rectangular hole for the wiring board's 10-pin box
header, whose face touches the inside of the side, so a ribbon cable plugs
in from outside.
