A lid that fits on top of the base box (LS1/base), like the PB6 lid. It
extends past the box's breadth by its own thickness on each side and runs
down the outside of the box to the bottom of the base. In the longitudinal
direction it extends a = 5 mm past the box at both ends. Under the lid, two
lips sit at the front (+x) and the back (-x), just inside the base's end
walls; there are no lips along the sides (+-y).

An 8 x 1 RGB WS2812 stick sits against the inside of the lid's +y side,
along it, with its LEDs facing out, each LED seated in its own square window
through the side. It is screwed to two little pillars on the inside of the
side with M1.2 countersunk screws.
