A small clamp bar that lies across the USB-C breakout's receptacle, just
behind the front wall, and is screwed down at both ends to two posts in the
base beside the board with M1.2 countersunk screws, pressing the connector
down so the board stays put when a plug goes in and out. It
reaches back past the receptacle's metal shell, and a lip comes down behind
the shell, close to the board, so the receptacle is held well when a plug
is pushed in.
