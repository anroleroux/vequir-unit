A clamp that holds the tops of the two standing boards, the wiring board
and the TP4056 charger. It is a bar across the top of a pillar that rises
between the two boards, screwed down to it with one M1.2 x 6 countersunk
Phillips screw, with a slot at either end that the top edge of each board
sits in, so the clamp presses both boards down into their slots in the
floor.
