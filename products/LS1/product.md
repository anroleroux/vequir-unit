# LS1

The LS1: a single-cell 18650 unit in a box like the PB6's, with a one-cell
protection board, an MT3608 boost regulator, a USB-C breakout, a Raspberry
Pi Pico 2 W that reads the cell's voltage, a lit push button on its back
wall, a buzzer, a DS18B20 temperature sensor and a row of eight WS2812 RGB LEDs showing through square
windows in the lid's side. Its enclosure, a base and a lid, is built
from plain-language descriptions as CadQuery B-rep code, and one assembly
places them with the bought-in parts.
